## Flappy MIPT
Hi! If you got here, you can check out the game, which was prepared 
and developed by me and my team in 2 weeks (actually 2 nights).

You play as a little <u>phystech</u>. Your goal is to live as long as possible and not be expelled.
To do this, you need to avoid obstacles and shoot back from the forces of evil.
